GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE

University Court APSU ka supreme governing body hai, jiske paas Executive Council aur Academic Council ke acts ko override karne ki power hai.

Executive Council University ka Chief Executive body hai. Isme Vice-Chancellor, Madhya Pradesh Government ke Education Department ke Secretary (ya unke nominee), Court dwara elected do members, Chancellor dwara nominate kiye gaye do members, Chancellor dwara seniority ke hisaab se rotate hone wale char College Principals, aur Chancellor dwara nominate kiye gaye char Deans shamil hote hain. Executive Council University ke funds control karta hai aur regulations banata hai.

Academic Council University ka Chief Academic body hai. Ye Faculties aur Boards of Studies constitute karta hai. Boards of Studies papers aur syllabi taiyar karte hain. Academic Council admission aur examination ke execution se related ordinances aur regulations bhi recommend karta hai.

Madhya Pradesh ke Governor University ke Chancellor hote hain (statutory structure ke according).

CENTRAL FACILITIES

Central Library: University ki central library mein books aur journals ka bada collection hai, saath hi e-library section bhi hai jisse online journals, books aur publications access ki ja sakti hain. Har teaching department apni departmental library bhi maintain karta hai.

University Publications: University ke do regular publications hain - Vindhya Bharti (annual multi-disciplinary research journal) aur Vishwavidyalaya Saptahiki (weekly newsletter).

Hostel Facility: Campus mein boys aur girls ke liye ek-ek hostel hai. Seats limited hain aur quota har department ko allot hota hai.

University Guest House: Campus mein hai, guests ke liye nominal cost par accommodation aur catering available hai.

University Stadium and Sports: Stadium city ke central area mein hai, jisme Cricket, Volleyball, Basketball, Kho-Kho, Kabaddi aur Wrestling ki facilities hain.

University Auditorium: Founder Vice-Chancellor Pt. Shambhu Nath Shukla ke naam par hai, 500+ seating capacity ke saath.

NSS: Do NSS (National Service Scheme) units campus mein active hain.

APSU Incubation Centre: Startups ke liye training aur support deta hai, Section 8 Company ke roop mein registered.

Directorate of Skill Development: Employability badhane ke liye alag-alag Certificate Programmes offer karta hai, jo University ke teaching departments ke through run hote hain.

Study Centre of IGNOU: Indira Gandhi National Open University ka study centre bhi campus mein hai, jiske co-ordinator Prof. Shrikant Mishra hain.

Bank aur Post Office: Indian Bank aur Post Office ki branches campus mein hain.

Primary Health Centre: Campus mein emergency first-aid ke liye ek Primary Health Centre hai.
