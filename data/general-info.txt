This file contains: APSU Rewa ki basic identity, establishment, recognition, location, campus, aur recent initiatives ki jaankari.

UNIVERSITY IDENTITY

University ka poora naam Awadhesh Pratap Singh University hai, jise APSU ya APSU Rewa bhi kaha jata hai. Ye Madhya Pradesh ki ek state university hai, Rewa mein sthit. APSU ek teaching-cum-affiliating university hai - matlab ye khud teaching bhi karti hai (apne 16 departments ke through) aur saath hi Madhya Pradesh ke kai districts ke colleges ko affiliate bhi karti hai.

ESTABLISHMENT

University 20 July 1968 ko Madhya Pradesh ki state university ke roop mein establish hui thi, aur usi din se first classes shuru hui. University ka naam Captain Awadhesh Pratap Singh ke naam par rakha gaya hai, jo ek freedom fighter aur national leader the, jinhone Vindhya region mein educational opportunities ki zaroorat mehsoos ki thi. Unke bete Shri Govind Narayan Singh, jo baad mein 1967 mein Madhya Pradesh ke Chief Minister bane, unhone is idea ko revive kiya - us waqt 30 lakh rupaye is purpose ke liye rakhe gaye the.

RECOGNITION

University ko UGC (University Grants Commission) ne February 1972 mein recognize kiya tha. University Association of Indian Universities (AIU) aur Association of Commonwealth Universities (ACAU) ki member hai.

University ko NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council) ne 'B++' grade se accredit kiya hai, jo 28 February 2029 tak valid hai.

AICTE (All India Council for Technical Education) ka approval APSU ke specific technical programmes (jaise MBA, MCA) ke liye programme-wise hota hai - poori university ko blanket "AICTE affiliated" nahi kehna chahiye.

LOCATION

APSU Rewa, Madhya Pradesh mein located hai, pincode 486003. University campus Sirmour Road par, Rewa city se lagbhag 3 kilometre north mein located hai.

CAMPUS

University ka campus 258 acres mein phaila hua hai, hilly aur green area mein.

Campus mein ye buildings/facilities hain: Administrative Block; Department of Environmental Biology; Department of Physics; Department of Chemistry; Department of Mathematics & English; Humanities Block; Department of Psychology; Ambedkar Bhawan; Life Long Learning Department; AntarBharti Building; Community Hall; Biotechnology Building; Law & Commerce Building; Directorate of Distance Education Building; Department of Business Administration; Computer Centre; USIC Building; Library; Pt. Shambhu Nath Shukla Auditorium; Guest House; Faculty Hostel Building; Gymnasium Hall; Yoga Hall; Women's Common Room; Indian Bank; Boys Hostel aur Girls Hostel; Sanjiwa Clinic & Health Centre; MP Online Kiosk & Photocopy Centre.

Campus mein university employees ke bachchon aur nearby locality ke liye ek well-equipped Inter Mediate School bhi establish kiya gaya hai, university ki social responsibility ke ek hisse ke roop mein.

ACADEMIC STRUCTURE

University mein 16 teaching departments aur 12 Study Centres hain, jo regular aur self-supporting basis par undergraduate, postgraduate, diploma aur certificate programmes offer karte hain. University ki jurisdiction mein 200 se zyada affiliated colleges hain, jo Rewa, Satna, Sidhi, Singrauli, Mauganj aur Maihar districts mein hain. University 30 se zyada certificate programmes bhi youth ki skill development ke liye offer karta hai.

RECENT INITIATIVES

University ne apni sanskritik aur intellectual heritage ko preserve karne ke liye teen naye centres banaye hain: Ramayana Shodh Peeth, Maharaja Martand Singh Baghel Shodh Peeth, aur Bharatiya Gyan Parampara Kendra. University ne innovation aur entrepreneurship badhane ke liye APSU Incubation Centre bhi banaya hai (ek Section 8 Company ke roop mein registered), jisne Beetle Nut Toys, Handmade Soaps aur Tribal Art jaise startups ko training di hai. University ne pichhle saal 33 nayi Undergraduate, Graduate, Diploma aur Certificate programmes launch ki thi, aur is saal PG Diploma in Water Conservation aur Gujarati/Sindhi languages mein certificate programmes bhi introduce kiye hain. University 58 saal se central India mein ek premier higher education institution ke roop mein khada hai.

ABOUT REWA CITY

Rewa ko "Land of White Tigers" kaha jata hai - duniya ka pehla white tiger yahan mila tha, jiska naam Mohan tha (ruling king Maharaja Martand Singh ne rakha). Rewa central Vindhya plateau par sthit hai, aur 17th century se Princely State of Rewa ki capital raha hai. "Rewa" naam Narmada nadi ke prachin naam "Reva" se aaya hai. Rewa Madhya Pradesh ka ek divisional headquarter hai aur Central India mein education aur culture ka important centre hai.

CONNECTIVITY

Rewa Satna se lagbhag 52 km door hai. Prayagraj aur Jabalpur se four-lane roads se juda hai. Rewa railway se bhi juda hai, lagbhag 11 trains yahan se chalti hain, jisme New Delhi, Mumbai, Bhopal, Indore, Jabalpur, Bilaspur, Nagpur, Kevadia aur Rajkot jaise shehron ke liye direct trains shamil hain. Paas ke tourist spots mein White Tiger Safari (Mukundpur), Bandhavgarh, Khajuraho, Maihar, Chitrakoot, aur Vindhyavasini shamil hain.
