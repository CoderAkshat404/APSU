This file contains: APSU Rewa ki admission procedure, reservations, scholarships, attendance requirements, aur general instructions.

ADMISSION PROCEDURE

Certificate, UG aur PG Programmes mein admission university level par diya jata hai, qualifying examination mein mile marks ke aadhar par. Madhya Pradesh Higher Education Department, Bhopal ki guidelines strictly follow ki jaati hain.

MBA Programme ka admission CMAT (AICTE dwara conduct kiya jata hai) ke through hota hai. Admission counselling Director, Technical Education (DTE), Bhopal (MP) dwara ki jaati hai.

MPharm, BPharm aur DPharm programmes ka admission bhi Director, Technical Education, Bhopal (MP) dwara conduct hota hai.

NCTE approved BEd, BPEd aur MPEd programmes ka admission Higher Education Department, Madhya Pradesh, Bhopal (MP) dwara centrally conduct hota hai.

Provisionally admitted candidates ki list concerned Department/Study Centre ke notice board par display hoti hai. Applicants ko apne admission status ke baare mein department se regularly touch mein rehna chahiye. Agar koi candidate diye gaye time mein report karke admission lene mein fail hota hai, to uski candidature agli list mein consider ki jaati hai agar vacancies available hon.

RESERVATIONS

SC/ST/OBC, Girls, Ex-Military, Freedom Fighters, aur University Employees jaise reserved categories ko University aur State Government ke guidelines/rules ke according admission diya jata hai. Reserved category seats claim karne wale candidates ko apne application form ke saath competent authority se attested certificate ki copy submit karni hoti hai.

SCHOLARSHIPS

Reserved category ke UG aur PG students ko Post Metric Scholarship aur Financial Assistance for Residence (Awas Sahayata) provide ki jaati hai. UG Programmes ke meritorious students ko MMVY (Medhavi) scholarship di jaati hai. Economically poor students jo UG Programmes mein admit hain, unhe MMJKY (Sambal) scholarship di jaati hai. Girl students Gao ki Beti aur Pratibha Kiran Yojna ke liye bhi eligible hain.

ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS

Har candidate ko semester examination mein baithne ke liye minimum attendance requirements poori karni hoti hai. Har semester ki classes mein candidate ka kam se kam 75% attendance hona zaroori hai, warna uska examination form forward nahi kiya jayega.

PREVENTION OF RAGGING

University campus ke andar aur bahar dono jagah ragging totally banned hai. Har naye admitted student ko ensure karna hoga ki wo kisi doosre student ki ragging mein directly ya indirectly involved na ho. Newly admitted students ko kisi bhi ragging incident ki jaankari turant university administration ko deni chahiye. Ragging mein involved paye gaye students ke against strict disciplinary action li jaati hai. Anti-Ragging Squad aur Anti-Ragging Committee campus mein ragging prevent karne ke liye active hain aur strict action lete hain.

PREVENTION OF SEXUAL HARASSMENT

Internal Complaint Committee campus mein sexual harassment ke cases ke against strict action lene ke liye active hai.

YOUTH FESTIVAL

Har saal Youth Festival organize hota hai, jo students ke talent aur skills showcase karta hai aur unke overall development mein madad karta hai. Government ke directives ke hisaab se, Youth Festival char levels par organize hota hai: Inter Class-College Level (UTD), Inter College-District Level, Inter District-University Level, aur Inter University-State Level. Har level par baais (22) competitive events hote hain academics, dance, singing, theatre aur fine arts se related. Youth Festival ka calendar Higher Education Department, Government of Madhya Pradesh dwara declare kiya jata hai.

MP ONLINE AUR CRISP

Admission, examination, aur TC/Migration/Degree se related sabhi applications MP Online aur CRISP ke through online process ki jaati hain.

CASHLESS TRANSACTION

University mein cash transactions allowed nahi hain. Saari transactions online hoti hain MP Online aur CRISP portal ke through, ya NEFT, RTGS aur Bank Demand Drafts ke through jahan zaroorat ho.

ADMISSION STATUS

Agar student apne individual application status, merit position, ya admission confirmation ke baare mein pooche, to agent student ke personal application record ko access nahi kar sakta jab tak authorised system integration available na ho. Aise questions ke liye student ko concerned department ya admission office se verify karna chahiye.

WEBSITE

University ki apni website hai: http://www.apsurewa.ac.in. University se related saari relevant information regularly website par update ki jaati hai. Admission, examination, seminar, conference, workshop se related notifications website par available hoti hain.

AGENT RESPONSE RULE

Agar student admission ke baare mein aisi information pooche jo yahan available nahi hai (jaise exact admission dates, exact document checklist, ya exact application fee), agent ko koi andaza ya galat jawab nahi dena chahiye. Agent ko honestly kehna chahiye: "Ye jaankari mere paas abhi available nahi hai. Kripya APSU ki latest admission notice check karein ya admission office se confirm karein."
