DEPARTMENT-WISE PROGRAMME MAPPING (Regular vs Self-Financing)

Ye section batata hai ki har department "Regular" (kam fee wale, university-funded) aur "Self-Financing" (self-supporting, zyada fee wale) programmes mein se kya-kya offer karta hai.

Ancient Indian History, Culture & Archaeology: Regular mein MA aur PhD (AIHC&A) aur PhD (History) hai. Self-Financing mein MBA Tourism Administration, BBA Honors Tourism and Hospitality, BA Honors AIHC&A, Diploma in Field Archeology, PG Diploma in Museology, MA/BA Jainology, aur MA/BA Prakrut hai.

Business Administration: Regular mein MBA Dual Specialization aur PhD (Business Administration) hai. Self-Financing mein BBA Honors (HR/Finance/Marketing/Event Management) hai.

Business Economics: Regular mein MA aur PhD (Business Economics) hai. Self-Financing mein BA Honors Economics, BCom Honors, BCom (Retail Operations Management) hai. Ye department BALLB Honors aur LLM ke liye bhi Nodal Department hai.

Chemistry: Regular mein MSc aur PhD (Chemistry) hai. Self-Financing mein MSc aur BSc Honors Bio-Chemistry, BSc Honors Chemistry, aur BPharm/DPharm hai.

Computer Applications: Isme koi Regular programme nahi hai. Self-Financing mein MCA, BCA, Diploma in Hardware and Networking, Diploma in Web Designing, aur Diploma in Cloud Based Application and Networking hai.

Computer Science: Regular mein PGDCA aur PhD (Computer Science) hai. Self-Financing mein MSc aur BSc Honors Computer Science, MTech (CS-AI&ML), BTech (CSE-Data Science), PG Diploma in Data Science, Diploma Course in Python, Diploma Course in Cyber Security, aur PG Diploma in Generative AI and Autonomous Agents hai.

English: Regular mein MA aur PhD (English) hai. Self-Financing mein BA Honors English aur Diploma in Translation Studies hai.

Environmental Biology: Regular mein MSc aur PhD (Env. Biology), PhD (Bio-Technology), aur PhD (Micro-Biology) hai. Self-Financing mein MSc/BSc Honors Bio-Technology, MSc/BSc Honors Micro-Biology, BSc Honors/MSc Botany, BSc Honors/MSc Zoology, BSc Agriculture Science, aur Diploma in Nutrition and Dietician hai.

Hindi: Regular mein MA aur PhD (Hindi) hai. Self-Financing mein BA Honors Hindi aur Certificate Programme in Gujarati Language hai.

Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Policy Research: Isme koi Regular programme nahi hai. Self-Financing mein BA (Political Science) hai.

Life Long Learning: Regular mein PhD (Education) hai. Self-Financing mein MBA HRD, PG Diploma in Rural Management, aur BEd hai.

Mathematical Sciences: Regular mein MSc aur PhD (Mathematics) hai. Self-Financing mein BSc Honors Mathematics aur PG Diploma in Vedic Mathematics hai.

Philosophy: Regular mein MA aur PhD (Philosophy) hai. Self-Financing mein BA Honors Philosophy, BA Honors/MA Sociology, MSW, aur Diploma in Life Values in Vedant hai. Ye department Diploma in Vocal Music ke liye bhi Nodal Department hai.

Physics: Regular mein MSc aur PhD (Physics) hai. Self-Financing mein BSc Honors Physics aur BTech (Mining and Mining Survey) hai. Ye department Diploma in Women Empowerment and Development ke liye bhi Nodal Department hai.

Physical Education & Sports: Regular mein PhD (Physical Education) hai. Self-Financing mein MPEd, BPEd, BPES, MA Yoga/DYEd, aur PG Diploma in Naturopathy and Yogic Science hai.

Psychology: Regular mein MA aur PhD (Psychology) hai. Self-Financing mein MBA IR&PM, PGDGC, MA Clinical Psychology/BA Honors Psychology, PG Diploma in Child Psychology and Family Counselling, aur PG Diploma in Mental Health and Stress Management hai.

Note: "Nodal Department" ka matlab hai ki wo programme kisi doosre department ke academic supervision ke under offer hota hai, jabki wo programme khud kisi aur department se related lag sakta hai.

KNOWN CONFLICT - MSW, SOCIOLOGY, MA YOGA, AUR DYEd KI DEPARTMENT MAPPING

Do alag sources mein alag jaankari mili hai in programmes ke baare mein:
- "Department-wise Programmes at a Glance" table ke according, MSW, BA Honors/MA Sociology, aur Diploma in Life Values in Vedant Philosophy department ke Self-Financing programmes hain.
- Lekin Department of Psychology ke apne profile page ke according, Psychology department khud MSW, BA Sociology, MA Yoga, aur DYEd (Diploma in Yoga Education) ke liye "nodal department" hai (Study Centre for Industrial Relations and Personal Management aur Study Centre for Social Work ke through).

Ye ek genuine conflict hai bulletin ke andar hi. Agar student in courses (MSW, Sociology, MA Yoga, DYEd) ke exact department ke baare mein pooche, to agent ko koi ek version confidently nahi batana chahiye. Agent ko kehna chahiye: "Is course ke exact department ke baare mein mere paas do alag jaankariyan hain, is confusion se bachne ke liye kripya concerned department ya admission office se confirm karein."


ADMISSION MODE NOTE

MBA Programme (AICTE Approved) ka admission CMAT aur Director, Technical Education (DTE), Bhopal (MP) ke through hota hai. BEd, BPEd aur MPEd Programmes (NCTE Approved) ka admission Madhya Pradesh Higher Education Department, Bhopal (MP) ke through hota hai. MPharm, BPharm aur DPharm Programmes (PCI approved) ka admission Director, Technical Education (DTE), Madhya Pradesh, Bhopal (MP) ke through hota hai.
