APSU TEACHING DEPARTMENTS - OVERVIEW

APSU Rewa mein 16 teaching departments hain: Ancient Indian History Culture & Archaeology, Business Administration, Business Economics, Chemistry, Computer Applications, Computer Science, English, Environmental Biology, Hindi, Life Long Learning, Mathematical Sciences, Philosophy, Psychology, Foreign Languages, Physical Education & Sports Science, aur Physics.

DEPARTMENT OF ANCIENT INDIAN HISTORY CULTURE & ARCHAEOLOGY
==================================================

Department September 1984 mein 6th Five-Year Plan ke under establish hua tha. UGC dwara sanctioned teaching posts mein ek professor, ek reader, aur teen lecturers shamil the.

Department PhD aur MA (AIHC & Arch.) regular programmes ke roop mein conduct karta hai. BA (Hons) Tourism programme Self-Supporting Programme (SSP) ke roop mein offer hota hai.

Department mein ek museum hai (1991 mein establish hua), jisme "Bouddha" mantra aur "Kalma" ki architectural stupa sahit epigraphical/paleographical remains, pottery, paintings, excavated materials, aur rare fossils/prehistoric tools hain. Museum sabke liye khula hai.

Department ke paas Rich Departmental Library hai (Human Values par centred, ~6000 books, Sanskrit/Hindi/Urdu manuscripts, dissertations, thesis) aur Computer Facility (archaeological/photographic/scientific equipment ke saath) hai.

Study Centre for Tourism 1991 mein establish hua, MBA Tourism Administration aur BBA Tourism and Hospitality Management offer karta hai. Special emphasis tour/travel management, tourism marketing, heritage management, hotel management, financial management par hai. Job training mandatory hai. Department har saal World Tourism Day par Tourism Festival organize karta hai.

COURSE AND DEPARTMENT INFORMATION RULE
==================================================

Agar student kisi course ka naam pooche, agent ko courses-list.txt mein available exact programme information check karni chahiye.

Agar student kisi department ka naam pooche (history, HOD, faculty), agent ko is file (department-details.txt) mein available information check karni chahiye.

Fee ke questions ke liye agent ko fee-structure.txt ki information follow karni chahiye - is file mein fee nahi hai.

Agar kisi programme ka department mapping verified nahi hai ya conflict hai (jaise MSW/Sociology/Yoga), agent ko ek confident answer nahi dena chahiye - dono versions mention karke confirm karne ko kehna chahiye.

Agar information knowledge base mein available nahi hai, agent ko koi andaza nahi lagana chahiye aur fallback-instructions.txt ke according response dena chahiye.
